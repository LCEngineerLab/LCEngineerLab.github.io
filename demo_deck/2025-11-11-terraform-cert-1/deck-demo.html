What is Terraform Associate Certification?

Validates knowledge of core Terraform concepts and skills for production use.

Demonstrates ability to:

  • Understand infrastructure as code (IaC)
  • Write and maintain Terraform configurations
  • Manage Terraform state
  • Work with Terraform modules
  • Understand workflows and best practices

Highly valued in DevOps and cloud engineering

Exam Format

Duration: 1 hour (60 minutes)

Questions: ~57 (Multiple choice & select)

Passing Score: 70% or higher

Language: English

Cost: ~$70.50 USD

Format: Proctored online exam

Exam Topics (9 Domains)

  • IaC concepts (15-20%)
  • Terraform's purpose (10-15%)
  • Terraform basics (20-25%)
  • Terraform CLI (15-20%)
  • Terraform modules (15-20%)
  • Terraform workflow (15-20%)
  • State management (10-15%)
  • Configuration management (15-20%)
  • Terraform Cloud/Enterprise (5-10%)

Registration: Step 1

Create HashiCorp Account

  • Visit HashiCorp Certification Portal
  • Click "Get Started" or "Register for Exam"
  • Create or sign in to HashiCorp account

Registration: Step 2

Schedule Your Exam

  • Navigate to Terraform Associate page
  • Click "Schedule Exam"
  • Redirected to PSI platform
  • Create PSI account if needed
  • Select exam date and time
  • Complete payment

Registration: Step 3

Prepare for Exam Day

  • System: Stable internet, webcam, quiet environment
  • ID: Valid government-issued ID
  • Environment: Proctoring software verifies before exam
  • No Notes: Cannot use external resources

Registration: Step 4

Take the Exam

  • Log in 15 minutes before scheduled time
  • Complete system check and verification
  • Exam begins after verification
  • Results immediately after completion
