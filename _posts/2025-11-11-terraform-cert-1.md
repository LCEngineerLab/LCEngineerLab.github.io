## What is the Terraform Associate Certification?

The HashiCorp Certified: Terraform Associate certification validates your knowledge of the core concepts and skills required to use Terraform effectively in production environments. It's an entry-level certification that demonstrates your ability to:

- Understand infrastructure as code (IaC) concepts
- Write and maintain Terraform configurations
- Manage Terraform state
- Work with Terraform modules
- Understand Terraform workflows and best practices

This certification is highly valued in the DevOps and cloud engineering communities, as Terraform has become the standard for infrastructure provisioning across major cloud providers.

## Exam Overview

### Exam Format

The Terraform Associate exam is a **proctored online exam** that you can take from the comfort of your home or office. Here are the key details:

- **Duration**: 1 hour (60 minutes)
- **Question Type**: Multiple choice and multiple select questions
- **Number of Questions**: Approximately 57 questions
- **Passing Score**: You need to achieve a score of 70% or higher to pass
- **Language**: The exam is available in English
- **Cost**: The exam fee is typically around $70.50 USD (prices may vary by region)

### Exam Topics

The exam covers the following domains:

1. **Understand Infrastructure as Code (IaC) concepts** (15-20%)
2. **Understand Terraform's purpose** (10-15%)
3. **Understand Terraform basics** (20-25%)
4. **Use the Terraform CLI** (15-20%)
5. **Interact with Terraform modules** (15-20%)
6. **Navigate Terraform workflow** (15-20%)
7. **Implement and maintain state** (10-15%)
8. **Read, generate, and modify configuration** (15-20%)
9. **Understand Terraform Cloud and Enterprise capabilities** (5-10%)

## Registration Process

### Step 1: Create a HashiCorp Account

1. Visit the [HashiCorp Certification Portal](https://www.hashicorp.com/certification/terraform-associate)
2. Click on "Get Started" or "Register for Exam"
3. Create a HashiCorp account if you don't have one, or sign in with your existing account

### Step 2: Schedule Your Exam

1. Once logged in, navigate to the Terraform Associate certification page
2. Click "Schedule Exam"
3. You'll be redirected to the PSI (exam delivery partner) platform
4. Create an account on the PSI platform if needed
5. Select your preferred exam date and time
6. Complete the payment process

### Step 3: Prepare for Exam Day

- **System Requirements**: Ensure you have a stable internet connection, a webcam, and a quiet environment
- **ID Verification**: Have a valid government-issued ID ready (driver's license or passport)
- **Environment Check**: The proctoring software will verify your environment before the exam starts
- **No Notes**: You cannot use notes, books, or any external resources during the exam

### Step 4: Take the Exam

- Log in to the PSI platform 15 minutes before your scheduled time
- Complete the system check and environment verification
- The exam will begin once verification is complete
- You'll receive your results immediately after completing the exam

## 報名流程

### 步驟 1：建立 HashiCorp 帳號

1. 訪問 [HashiCorp 認證入口網站](https://www.hashicorp.com/certification/terraform-associate)
2. 點擊「開始使用」或「報名考試」
3. 如果你還沒有 HashiCorp 帳號，請建立一個，或使用現有帳號登入

### 步驟 2：安排考試時間

1. 登入後，導航至 Terraform Associate 認證頁面
2. 點擊「安排考試」
3. 你將被重定向到 PSI（考試交付合作夥伴）平台
4. 如需要，在 PSI 平台上建立帳號
5. 選擇你偏好的考試日期和時間
6. 完成付款流程

### 步驟 3：準備考試當天

- **系統要求**：確保你有穩定的網路連接、網路攝影機和安靜的環境
- **身份驗證**：準備好有效的政府核發的身份證件（駕照或護照）
- **環境檢查**：監考軟體將在考試開始前驗證你的環境
- **禁止筆記**：考試期間不能使用筆記、書籍或任何外部資源

### 步驟 4：參加考試

- 在預定時間前 15 分鐘登入 PSI 平台
- 完成系統檢查和環境驗證
- 驗證完成後考試將開始
- 完成考試後你將立即收到結果
