## What is an Exchange?

An **exchange** is a marketplace where people can buy and sell assets. In the cryptocurrency space, exchanges can be divided into two main types:

**CEX (Centralized Exchange)**: Examples include Binance, OKX, and Coinbase.
- Trades are matched by the platform, and funds are held by the exchange.

**DEX (Decentralized Exchange)**: Examples include Uniswap, Hyperliquid, and dYdX.
- Trades are executed on the blockchain through smart contracts, and users maintain custody of their own funds.

## What is Leverage Trading?

**Leverage trading** involves borrowing money to amplify your trading position. For example:

- You have $100 USD.
- You open a 10x leverage long position.
- This means you're actually trading a $1,000 position (you've borrowed $900).

✅ **Advantages**: If the price rises by 10%, you make $100 (a 100% return on your capital).

❌ **Risks**: If the price drops by 10%, you get "liquidated" (forced to close your position), and your capital goes to zero.

This type of trading is typically implemented through **perpetual futures** or **margin trading**.

## What are Aster and Hyperliquid?

#### Aster

Aster is an emerging **decentralized trading platform (DEX)** or **perpetual futures platform** (similar to dYdX).

The goal is to enable users to conduct leverage trading on the blockchain with low latency and high liquidity.

It often combines AI trading assistance, quantitative strategies, and market prediction tools.

The underlying philosophy is typically "AI + DeFi."

#### Hyperliquid

Hyperliquid is a rapidly growing **Layer-1 DEX for Perpetuals** (a perpetual futures exchange built on its own chain).

Key features:

- Efficient matching engine (similar speed to CEX)
- Supports leverage trading (both long and short positions)
- Users maintain self-custody of funds (non-custodial)

In simple terms, it's a **decentralized version of Binance Futures**.

## nof1 AI Trading Competition

**nof1** is a competition platform that combines AI with trading strategies.

Participants (or teams) design AI models to trade automatically, for example, on platforms like Hyperliquid.

The system scores participants based on metrics such as return rate, stability, and risk management.

It's somewhat like an "AI Trading League," where AI systems compete against each other.

**Goals:**

- Identify the strongest AI trading agent
- Advance AI-driven automated finance (Autonomous Trading)

## Spot vs. Leverage Position: Key Differences

| Aspect | Spot (Spot Trading) | Leverage / Perpetual (Leverage/Contract) |
|--------|---------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Asset Ownership | You actually own the coins | You hold a contract, not the actual coins |
| Risk | No liquidation risk | Can be liquidated due to price volatility |
| Available Leverage | No leverage (1x) | Can use 2x~100x leverage |
| Fees | One-time trading fee | Includes funding rate (Funding Rate) |
| Holding Period | Unlimited | Perpetual contracts require maintaining margin |

#### In Simple Terms:

**Spot position**: You buy 1 ETH, and you actually own 1 ETH.

**Leverage position**: You bet that ETH will rise or fall, amplifying both returns and risks, but you don't own the actual ETH.

## 什麼是交易所 (Exchange)

交易所 是讓人們可以買賣資產的市場。
在加密貨幣領域，交易所可以分成兩種：

- CEX（Centralized Exchange，中心化交易所）：例如 Binance、OKX、Coinbase。
→ 交易由平台撮合、資金放在交易所裡。

- DEX（Decentralized Exchange，去中心化交易所）：例如 Uniswap、Hyperliquid、dYdX。
→ 交易在區塊鏈上透過智能合約進行，使用者自己保管資金。

## 什麼是槓桿交易 (Leverage Trading)

槓桿交易 是借錢放大你的交易部位。
例如：

你有 $100 美金。

你開 10x 槓桿 的多單（Long）。

代表你實際操作的是 $1,000 的部位（借了 $900）。

✅ 優點：如果價格漲 10%，你賺 $100（報酬率 100%）。
❌ 風險：如果價格跌 10%，你就被「強制平倉」（liquidated），本金歸零。

這種交易通常透過 永續合約 (perpetual futures) 或 margin trading 實現。

## Aster 與 Hyperliquid 是什麼公司

#### Aster

是一個新興的 去中心化交易平台 (DEX) 或 永續合約平台（類似 dYdX）。

目標是讓用戶能在區塊鏈上以低延遲、高流動性進行槓桿交易。

常結合 AI 交易輔助、量化策略、市場預測工具。

背後理念多半是「AI + DeFi」。

#### Hyperliquid

是目前成長很快的 Layer-1 DEX for Perpetuals（建立自己鏈的永續合約交易所）。

特點：

- 高效的撮合引擎（類似 CEX 的速度）

- 支援槓桿交易（多空方向都可以）

- 用戶資金仍自我保管（non-custodial）

簡單來說，就是 去中心化版本的 Binance Futures。

## nof1 AI 交易比賽

nof1 是一個結合 AI 與交易策略的競賽平台。

參賽者（或團隊）會設計 AI 模型來自動交易，例如在 Hyperliquid 上操作。

系統會根據報酬率、穩定性、風險等指標評分。

有點像「AI Trading League」，讓 AI 彼此對戰。

目標：

- 找出最強的 AI 交易 agent。

- 推動 AI 驅動的自動化金融（Autonomous Trading）。

## Spot vs. Leverage Position 的區別

| 項目 | Spot (現貨) | Leverage / Perpetual (槓桿/合約) |
|------|-------------|----------------------------------|
| 資產所有權 | 你真正擁有幣 | 你持有的是合約，不擁有幣 |
| 風險 | 不會被強制平倉 | 可能因價格波動被清算 |
| 可用槓桿 | 無槓桿（1x） | 可用 2x~100x 等槓桿 |
| 手續費 | 一次性交易費 | 有資金費率 (Funding Rate) |
| 持倉時間 | 無限期 | 永續合約需維持保證金 |

簡單說：

- Spot position：你買了 1 顆 ETH，就真的有 1 顆。

- Leverage position：你押注 ETH 會漲或跌，放大報酬與風險，但不擁有實際 ETH。
